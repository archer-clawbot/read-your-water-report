Making Sense of Hardness Results

Hardness mostly reflects dissolved calcium and magnesium. It's generally treated as an aesthetic and maintenance issue (scale, spotting, soap performance) rather than a health one.

Units you'll see

Reports may express hardness in mg/L (or ppm) as calcium carbonate, or in grains per gallon. Labels like "soft" or "very hard" are attached to ranges, and the exact cutoffs vary by source.

Rather than converting by hand, you can enter your number into a water hardness calculator to convert between units and see the category.

Does it need treatment?

That depends on how much scale bothers you and what it does to appliances and fixtures. For background on effects and options, see Water System Guide's page on hard water.
