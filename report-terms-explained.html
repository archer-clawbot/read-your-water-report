Water Report Terms Explained

mg/L and ppm
Milligrams per liter. For water, this is commonly treated as equivalent to parts per million.
µg/L and ppb
Micrograms per liter, roughly parts per billion. It's used for substances that matter at very small amounts.
gpg
Grains per gallon, a unit used mostly for hardness and by water softener sellers.
MCL
Maximum Contaminant Level, an enforceable limit for public water systems under U.S. drinking water rules.
Secondary standard
A non-enforceable guideline, usually about taste, odor, color or staining rather than health.
ND
Not detected. The substance was below the level the lab method can detect. It doesn't necessarily mean zero.
Reporting or detection limit
The lowest amount the lab reliably measures with its method.

Hardness is the result where units cause the most confusion. Water System Guide has a page on how water hardness is measured, and the hardness results page here covers the basics.
