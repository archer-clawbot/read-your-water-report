How to Read a Water Test Report

A lab report or annual water quality report can look like a wall of chemistry. Most of them follow the same pattern, and once you know it, they're much easier to read.

The typical layout

Each row usually lists a substance (the analyte), the amount found, the unit, and a reference value to compare it against. Some reports also flag results that exceed a limit.

Reading one row

Check the unit first. The same substance can be reported in different units, and comparing a result with a limit in another unit leads to wrong conclusions. Then compare the result with the reference value the report gives.

City water vs your own test

A utility's annual report describes water leaving the treatment system across the service area. Your tap can differ, for example because of household plumbing. A test at your own tap answers a different question.
