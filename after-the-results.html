What to Do After You Get Your Results

1. Confirm anything surprising

A single unexpected result is worth retesting before you spend money. Ask the lab whether resampling makes sense.

2. Match treatment to the specific finding

Different technologies target different problems. For example, a softener and a reverse osmosis unit solve very different things, as reverse osmosis vs a water softener lays out.

If drinking water is the concern, RO Explained walks through how reverse osmosis works.

3. Check certification claims

Ask which standard a product is certified to and for which contaminant, then confirm it in the certifier's listing. See Water System Guide's guide to NSF-certified water filters for how to check.

4. Retest after treatment

A follow-up test shows whether the system is doing what it should. Ask any installer, in writing, whether a post-installation test is included.
