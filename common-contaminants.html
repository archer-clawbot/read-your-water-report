Common Water Contaminants in Plain Language

Here is what common report entries generally refer to. For whether a specific result is a concern, compare it with the reference value on your report and ask your utility, lab or health department.

Disinfectants

Chlorine and chloramine are added by many utilities to keep water safe in the pipes. Chloramine lasts longer and affects filter choices differently than chlorine. Water System Guide explains chloramine in tap water.

Minerals and metals

Iron and manganese are mostly known for staining and taste. Lead and copper usually come from household plumbing rather than the source water, so results can vary by tap and how long water sat in the pipes.

Nutrients

Nitrate can come from fertilizer, septic systems or animal waste. It's a common concern for private wells in agricultural areas. The companion Well Water Primer covers well-specific issues.

Microbial

Total coliform and E. coli are indicators. A positive result signals that the water system may be letting contamination in, and it calls for prompt follow-up.
